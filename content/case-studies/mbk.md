## The problem

Client work at a regulated B2B consultancy ran across disconnected tools and manual steps. No one had a single view of a client, reporting to directors was done by hand, and every department had built its own workaround.

## What I did

I led the CRM transformation end to end, from discovery to rollout, giving the business one system for client work across departments.

## How I did it

- Spoke directly with clients and internal teams to find where onboarding and delivery broke down
- Ran an Agile-hybrid plan across five workstreams, with fortnightly director meetings and a RAID log to catch risks early
- Managed the CRM vendor and budget, and ran UAT with users and Product Owners
- Added Azure AI triage and a director dashboard, and automated stage-by-stage workflows with operations teams

## Trade-offs

- **Discovery before build.** Paused delivery to talk to clients first, because internal assumptions about friction were wrong.
- **Visibility before automation.** Mapped the full process before automating any of it.
- **MVP over everything at once.** Held scope requests against the approved baseline so the first release shipped on time.

## The result

The CRM MVP went live in 14 weeks. Automation removed around 3,800 manual hours a year across departments, and the work contributed £180K in won contract value.

*What I'd do differently: track workflow analytics from day one, so decisions during rollout could be made faster.*
