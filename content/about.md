## About me

I'm a product manager and former founder who loves taking messy, complex real-world problems and turning them into products that build user trust.

My path into product started by building things from scratch, founding ShasyMitra as an AI-enabled advisory and payments platform that scaled from zero to one to serve over 21,000 farmers across five Indian states. That experience taught me how to navigate heavy regulatory layers, build through ambiguity, and take total ownership of a product's lifecycle.

Seeking to sharpen my global lens, I moved to the UK to complete an MSc in International Business at Warwick on a merit scholarship before transitioning into the UK software market.

Today, I work as a Product Manager at MBK Early Years, where I own client accounts and design custom CRM systems and internal tools for a business serving thousands of customers. Across my career spanning payments, AI, and operations, I have learned that great products rely on finding human clarity inside operational chaos.

I am looking for my next role as a product manager in a team building at the intersection of fintech, compliance, and AI. If you are scaling a product that tackles complex, real-world problems and needs an owner who can take things from zero to one, let us talk.
