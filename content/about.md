## About me

Throughout my career so far, I've been drawn to the moment a business problem turns into a technology decision. That's where things often go wrong: the people who feel the problem and the people building the fix see it differently. My work is closing that gap, so the solution makes sense on both sides.

On the product side, I start with the people closest to the problem, work out what needs fixing, and shape it into something small enough to ship and useful enough to adopt. Building my own company taught me that deciding what not to build matters as much as what you do, and that trust is part of the product.

On the project side, I keep complex work moving with clear plans, honest risk tracking, steady communication with leadership and delivery teams, and scope that holds. Increasingly, I use AI to remove the manual work that slows teams down. What I enjoy most is the messy start of a project, when asking the right questions matters more than having the answers.
